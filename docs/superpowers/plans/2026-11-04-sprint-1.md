## Global constraints

Preserve uncommitted login work. Work in current feature checkout to extend that work. Do not merge/push or mark human review/testing complete. Server owns prices, totals, actor, timestamps and permissions. Cashier data is restricted to their shift. One payment SUCCESS and one invoice per order. Transfer reference unique across orders, exact amount required, confirmation actor/time stored. QR display never confirms receipt.

## Review focus

Concurrent/replayed confirmations must not duplicate inventory or invoice. Wrong owner/closed shift must never mutate. Failed confirmation must not consume a reference. Locked accounts must lose access on next request. Snapshot prices must survive later catalog changes.
